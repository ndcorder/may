HERESIARCH

A GAME OF CATEGORIES AND CONSEQUENCES

You are an Inquisitor of the Lexicon Conclave, dispatched to interrogate a heretic whose beliefs threaten the ordered categories of the world.

The heretic's crime is not what they have done. It is what they think.

Your task: discover what they believe. Understand it well enough to name it.

But be careful. The Conclave watches your words as closely as theirs. Question too deeply, show too much understanding, and you may find the interview turning in a direction you did not intend.
