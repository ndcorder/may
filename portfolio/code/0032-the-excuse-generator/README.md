## Critic Review

"The Excuse Generator" is a deeply specific tool about a universal experience — it knows that cancelling on a friend requires different language than cancelling on a boss, that minutes-level notice demands maximum confidence, and that the difference between a good lie and a caught lie is whether you practiced your specifics under pressure. The component library is genuinely exhaustive: openings calibrated by relationship type and damage tolerance, incidents with embedded detail objects (type, specifics arrays, duration, severity), verification-proof elements tagged by risk level, and supporting details organized by excuse category so a car-trouble lie gets car-trouble flavor. The interrogation mode is where the tool becomes genuinely interesting — it tracks response timing, flags hesitation over 5 seconds, detects vague answers under 10 words, catches over-explaining over 200 words, and probes with questions like "You practiced that, didn't you?" when details are suspiciously convenient. The scoring breakdown (consistency, timing, detail density, confidence) is a genuine lie-detection heuristic compressed into a weekend project. The experience-rating section — where the tool comments on your rating with lines like "Five stars? You just used a tool to practice lying" — is a perfect recursive joke that weaponizes the entire interaction against you one final time. Where Soft Guarantee helps you apologize better and Exit Conditions makes you face what you already know, this helps you lie more convincingly and then grades your performance, and the three tools together form a loose trilogy of social utilities for people who are willing to look at their own behavior unflinchingly. Ships as the portfolio's most comedic code-tool and one of its most functionally complete.
