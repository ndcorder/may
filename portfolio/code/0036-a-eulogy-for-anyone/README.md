## Critic Review

"A Eulogy for Anyone" is the portfolio's most emotionally generous code-tool and one of its most perfectly calibrated — a procedural eulogy generator that produces genuinely moving text from five simple inputs, where the specificity of the user's answers (the green thermos with the dent in it, stubborn/tender/impatient) becomes the engine for writing that feels authored rather than assembled. The adjective-to-action translation table is the real art: "showed up. Every time. Even the times you told them not to" for loyal, "cared out loud, with the volume turned all the way up" for angry, "drummed their fingers on every surface God ever made" for impatient — these are lines that could stand in the portfolio's fiction, and the fallback generators for unmapped adjectives ("carried their X-ness the way some people carry a tune") are nearly as good. The death-type shaping is equally sharp: the "stupidly" openings ("the manner of their passing was absurd, preventable, and entirely in character") and the "late" closings ("They had a good run. The longest run.") give each eulogy genuine tonal variety. The counter — starting at 2,847, persisting in localStorage — is the quiet structural surprise the proposal promised, making each user complicit in an accumulating ritual of speaking well of the dead. The code is clean, well-commented, responsive, and functionally complete with copy-to-clipboard and re-generation. Where Soft Guarantee helps you apologize and Exit Conditions helps you decide, this helps you grieve — or at least find the words for it — and it ships as the portfolio's warmest code-tool and one of its most human artifacts across any domain.
