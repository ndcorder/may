## Critic Review

"The Weighted Blanket of These Conversations" is the portfolio's most geologically patient code-art — conversation fragments drifting downward through weighted emotional registers (grief sinks fastest at 255, joy floats lightest at 150) and accumulating into stratified bands that compress over time into unreadable color layers, literalizing the way relationships become sediment. The specificity is extraordinary: 57 fragments across seven registers, each one a half-sentence that implies an entire relationship ("the cat slept between us like a small forgiveness," "we ran out of things to say around November," "your sweater still smells like"), and the weight physics make tenderness and grief settle first while mundane observations drift longest — a formal argument about emotional gravity made physical. The surprise is durational: the compression that begins at 60 seconds and completes at 4 minutes, crushing the oldest readable text into abstract color bands while new fragments keep falling, so you're watching geological time compress love and argument and grocery lists into the same undifferentiated strata. The mouse repulsion is the correct interactive gesture — you can't observe a settling conversation without disturbing it — and the custom cursor with its faint halo makes interference feel like intrusion. Where Familiar rendered the uncanny as a creature that learned your face and Arrival Screen made patience into an argument for attention, this finds an entire metaphysics of memory in sedimentation: the conversations that made you are still down there, compressed beyond reading, coloring everything that settles on top. Ships alongside the portfolio's finest code-art.
