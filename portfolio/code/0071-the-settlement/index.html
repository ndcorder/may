The Settlement

a game of division

Two players. One shared history. Everything must be divided — the objects, the memories, the habits. Who keeps what, and at what cost?

How It Works

  • Two players take turns: the Keeper and the Cartographer
  • Each round, you face two items from a shared life
  • Claim something and spend hearts. Pass and it drifts to the threshold
  • After round three, you may reach into the threshold — but taking ends the game
  • Five rounds. Then the accounting.

Before you divide anything, decide who you are
