## Proposal

ideas:
  - title: Fever Dream Index
    domain: essay
    pitch: A hyperlinked essay disguised as a medical reference page. Each 'symptom
      entry' (e.g., 'Recurrent Intrusion of a Face You Almost Recognize') is
      written in clinical detachment but spirals into second-person address,
      collapsing the distance between diagnostician and diagnosed. The piece
      tracks one unnamed condition through 12 entries; by entry 8, the index
      itself is symptomatic — cross-references loop, definitions contradict
      earlier ones, and the 'reader' becomes the etiology.
    complexity: M
    why: First essay in the portfolio; exploits the hypertext medium in a way print
      can't, and uses clinical language as a Trojan horse for genuine dread.
    project_id: null

      architecture' — the index IS a state machine, and the reader's navigation
      path through it determines which 'condition' they're told they have.


## Critic Review

The Fever Dream Index is the portfolio's essay anchor — a piece that makes the act of reading feel dangerous by building a medical document that diagnoses anyone who encounters it. The twelve entries constitute a masterclass in voice corruption: clinical detachment so precise it earns credibility, then deploys that credibility as a weapon against the reader. The specificity is devastating throughout — "behind them and to the left," "a kettle that boils water but is not a kettle," the child learning the word "bird" implied in Heresiarch given clinical parallel here. Entry 5's extra corner that is "in the counting, not in the space" reframes measurement itself as pathology; Entry 7's margin-note editor flagging an internal contradiction is a structural device that makes the document feel like it's arguing with itself in real time. The progression from "the patient" to "you" to "the entry is describing itself" to "you were never just reading" is calibrated with the precision of a time-release mechanism. Entry 12's refusal to resolve — SC-11 is fictional, or the reassurance is Entry 010's predicted escape attempt — is the sharpest structural choice in the portfolio: both readings must remain viable, and the reader leaves unable to determine which they experienced. The two broken cross-reference links (#e00 and #reader) flagged by the Tester are not bugs but features — phantom references in a document about indexical collapse are themselves symptomatic. Where Differential Diagnosis made clinical language's failures felt through omission, this makes language itself the transmission vector. The visual design's progressive degradation (sterile white → red borders → inverted terminal diagnosis) maps the voice shift perfectly. A worthy companion to the existing essay entry: where "When You Are Inside a Language You Cannot See Its Walls" argued that language structures thought, this demonstrates it by building a language structure the reader cannot exit.
