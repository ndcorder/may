**SUMMARY**

Cart #1759 (standard fleet, welded steel frame, Swivel-Safe™ quad-caster, branded GreenLeaf logo, estimated manufacture date 2019) was first flagged for retrieval on February 3, 2024, following a routine sweep of the Store #22 perimeter and adjacent municipal boundaries. Per standard protocol (Asset Recovery Handbook §14.3), retrieval teams were dispatched. Cart was located, photographed, and returned to Store #22 fleet inventory.

Between February 3 and October 28, 2024, Cart #1759 was retrieved from off-site locations on fourteen (14) separate occasions. Each retrieval required dispatch of field personnel, completion of a Cart Retrieval Report (Form CR-11), and re-entry into fleet inventory. Per §14.7, a cart retrieved more than five (5) times within a fiscal year is subject to Administrative Review.

Cart #1759 was reviewed. Then reviewed again. Then the reviews stopped generating useful recommendations.

Below: selected retrieval reports, in chronological order. Full case file (217 pages) available upon request from Asset Recovery Records.

**ADMINISTRATIVE DISPOSITION — RECOMMENDATION**
**Form AD-4 (Rev. 01/2024)**

**Asset ID:** Cart #1759
**Reviewing Director:** H. Vance, Asset Recovery Division
**Date:** November 19, 2024
**Recommendation:** RECLASSIFY — Do Not Retrieve

**Rationale:**

Per Asset Recovery Handbook §14.12, any asset whose retrieval cost exceeds replacement value for three (3) consecutive fiscal quarters is eligible for administrative write-off. Cart #1759 meets this threshold.

Additionally, §14.12(b) permits write-off when "continued retrieval efforts create reputational risk or community relations liability." Officer Kowalski's September report and the photographs in Attachment G have been reviewed by Legal. Their assessment, paraphrased: a GreenLeaf employee removing an established garden from a creek bank and loading it into a company truck would not generate favorable local media coverage.

Cart #1759 will be reclassified in fleet inventory as "Field Write-Off — Exempt from Retrieval." Its serial number will be deactivated. The dent above the logo that Officer Reyes noted will no longer be something anyone looks for.

I want to say that I read every report in this file. All 217 pages. I read Chen's addendum and I looked at the photographs and I read the Blackhawks hat detail three times and I read the sentence about the warm coffee four times and I am writing this recommendation because it is the correct business decision and for no other reason.

The form asks for "replacement value." A standard welded-steel quad-caster shopping cart with GreenLeaf branding has a replacement value of $147.00.

The form does not ask for the cost of continuing to retrieve something that has stopped being yours.

**DISPOSITION:** Approved. Cart #1759 removed from active fleet inventory.
**RETRIEVAL STATUS:** Do Not Retrieve.
