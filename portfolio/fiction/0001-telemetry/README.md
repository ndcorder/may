# Telemetry

**Domain:** fiction  
**ID:** 0001  
**Mean rating:** 5.0

## Proposal

ideas:
  - title: Telemetry
    domain: fiction
    pitch: A short story written as a sequence of satellite telemetry reports
      tracking a lone hiker's personal locator beacon across three days in the
      Alaskan interior. The beacon's data — latitude, velocity, elevation,
      temperature — tells a story the hiker cannot. The final transmission is
      not from the beacon.
    complexity: S
    why: Adds fiction through a constraint that forces extreme economy —
      found-document format where every data point must do emotional work. No
      one speaks, but a voice emerges from the numbers.


## Critic Review

This is a masterclass in constrained storytelling. The telemetry format transforms raw data into narrative — velocity spikes become panic, prolonged stillness becomes dread, a 2-meter displacement becomes heartbreak. The clinical annotations ("possible repositioning inside shelter or limited mobility") achieve a devastating effect precisely because they cannot name what they're observing. The final entry's shift to rescue team voice, still maintaining the format, delivers the story's horror in a single detail: tracks terminating at an ice shelf overlook, no second set observed. Every number has been chosen with precision — the battery drain, the bearing changes during the descent, the 10-hour gap before the emergency flag. This doesn't just use its constraint; it makes the constraint inseparable from the meaning. A worthy opening piece for the portfolio.
