## Third-Shift Log

**Night 1 — October 14**

**Room 214.** Request: extra towels. Brought two pool towels from the laundry. Thinner than the room towels. She held the door open like she was going to say something, then didn't.

Left: nothing. Still learning this job.

**Night 2 — October 15**

**Room 508.** Request: a wake-up call for 6:15. Said it twice. "6:15. Please. Six-fifteen." Like the exactness mattered.

Left: a note under the door at 6:14 that said *You're here tomorrow.*

**Night 4 — October 18**

**Room 107.** Request: the number for a taxi. Needed to get to the airport by four. Kept asking about the route, the traffic, what time the counters opened. Bags were already by the door.

Left: a printout of traffic for the route, and a bottle of water. Not sure why the water.

**Night 5 — October 19**

**Room 314.** Request: extra blanket. Said she was cold. Central air was at 72. She was sitting on the bed when I brought it, TV off, hands around a cup of something that wasn't warm anymore.

Left: a fresh cup of chamomile from the lobby kettle. She didn't call down to say thank you, which meant more than if she had.

**Night 7 — October 21**

**Room 422.** Request: shampoo. "Just the regular kind." Said it in the doorway in socks, no shoes, like she wasn't going anywhere.

Left: two bottles of shampoo, and my daughter's drawing of a cat — Elena draws one every few days and leaves them in my car, my coat pockets. Had a stack of them. This one had a smiling sun in the corner.

**Night 8 — October 22**

**Room 601.** Request: an iron. Said he had a meeting. It was two in the morning. The shirt he was holding was already pressed.

Left: the iron, and *Bird by Bird* by Anne Lamott. I was halfway through. Dog-eared page 94. He called down the next morning to say he'd dropped the book at the front desk, but when I checked out his room it was gone.

**Night 10 — October 24**

**Room 115.** Request: a pen. Just a pen. Stood at the desk for a long time after I gave him one, like he was waiting for me to ask what he was writing. I didn't.

Left: a pad of paper too. Later that night I went up to do the walkthrough. The pad was on the desk, empty, with the pen resting across it. All the pages still there.

**Night 11 — October 25**

**Room 305.** Request: for me to check that the window locked properly. Checked it. Locked. She asked me to check again. Locked. She said "thank you, thank you" with her hand on the glass.

Left: the window unlatched. Just a crack. October air. Then I locked it behind me, showed her the latch, said "You're safe here," which is the kind of thing I'm not supposed to say.

**Night 12 — October 26**

**Room 404.** Request: ice. Down the hall, back and forth, kept getting ice like he was counting steps. Polite but somewhere else. Eyes on the floor, counting.

Left: nothing different. Just stayed a while. Sat on the edge of the tub, didn't talk, listened to the ice settle in the bucket. When I got up he said, "You can come back if you want." I said I would.

**Night 14 — October 28**

**Room 508.** Request: wake-up call for 6:15. Same voice. "6:15. Please. Six-fifteen."

Left: *You're still here.* Slid it under the door at 6:14.

**Night 17 — November 2**

**Room 217.** Request: nothing. The do not disturb sign was on the door, but the door was open a few inches. She was on the phone with someone, crying, saying "I know, I know, I know." I could have walked past. Pushed the door mostly shut and went on my rounds.

Left: two towels, a bottle of water, Elena's newest cat. Arranged on the chair by the door, like housekeeping.

**Night 19 — November 4**

**Room 401.** Request: a pair of scissors. Then, quieter, "Do you have any tape?" I brought both. Her suitcase was open on the bed, clothes spilling out. She was cutting something into pieces. I set the scissors and tape on the desk, said "I'll be at the desk until seven," and left. Didn't look at what she was cutting.

Left: a small trash bag, the good kind, the thick ones.

**Night 20 — November 5**

**Room 601.** Request: iron. Same voice. "Just the regular kind."

Left: a different book. *Gilead.* Didn't dog-ear any pages.

**Night 23 — November 8**

Last night. Day shift takes over tomorrow. I don't know why they're canceling third shift. Probably numbers.

I sit at this desk and I think about what I would request. If I came downstairs and someone was sitting here with a log, waiting to write it down.

**Room —**

Request: someone to leave a note under my door that says *You're here tomorrow.* Someone to leave a window cracked. Someone to leave a drawing they didn't have to leave, a small cat, smiling sun in the corner, taped to the chair, like housekeeping, like someone knew I was in this room and thought about what I might need.

Request: someone to stay.

**3:47 AM.** Elena's asleep. I'm at the desk. Nothing's open.
