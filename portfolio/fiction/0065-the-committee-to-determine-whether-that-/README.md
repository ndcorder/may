## Critic Review

"Case No. 2024-4471" is the portfolio's most structurally perfect fiction since Exit Interview and one of its most emotionally devastating artifacts across any domain — a bureaucratic transcript in which five committee members adjudicate whether "see you around" constituted a goodbye, and in which the procedural frame (exhibits, motions, abstentions, dissents) becomes the architecture of how humans process ambiguity by forcing it into systems too small to contain it. The specificity is extraordinary and distributed with masterful restraint: the 450-620 millisecond pause, the comma splice as emotional evidence ("The comma is how his thumbs felt"), "Definitely" with a period described as "a wall," the committee's own case statistics (31 cases, 18 goodbyes, 9 not-goodbyes, 4 inconclusives) establishing this as institutional practice rather than absurdist premise. Each committee member's personal disclosure is distinct in texture and devastating in its particularity — Vasquez's Dr. Pham and the corgi named Walter, Jiang's "I'm glad it was you" and his uncertainty about whether he remembers the actual words anymore, Nolan's ten years spent keeping his own case permanently open, Reeves's voicemail pause that objectively doesn't lengthen but subjectively does, and Cole's 1987 wedding reception where Karen Wheeler danced with him twice and told him to leave and he has "never once wondered" except he has, he always has, his entire procedural resistance is the wonder. The surprise is structural and recursive: the committee rules "not a goodbye" while the reader understands the ruling is about the case in front of them and also about every case that isn't, and Cole's dissent — "she was clearly telling me to go. The committee should recognize that I've always known that" — is the artifact's most devastating line because the recognition he's requesting is not about Karen Wheeler. Where The Committee to Assign Blame for the Sunrise found theology in municipal minutes, this finds the entire architecture of romantic uncertainty in a hearing transcript — proof that the bureaucratic form, designed to resolve ambiguity, is itself the most honest document of how ambiguity actually feels: procedural, recursive, and never, ever adjourned. Ships alongside Telemetry, The Receipt, The Floor Plan, The River Trial, The Chore Wheel, and the Sunrise Committee as one of the finest artifacts the portfolio contains.
