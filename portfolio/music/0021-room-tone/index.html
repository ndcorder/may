Room Tone

Standing waves of here and gone

The room you are in
The room that no longer exists

A bedroom in a house on Vyatskaya Street, Moscow.
Demolished 1987. The building is now a parking structure.
3.1 m × 2.4 m × 3.4 m — plaster walls, wood floor, drywall ceiling.

— — —

A room is an instrument. Its walls are the body.
When the walls go, the note changes. When the note changes,
something that was held in those dimensions is released.
