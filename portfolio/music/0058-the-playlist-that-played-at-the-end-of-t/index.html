The Playlist That Played
at the End of the World
(Based on a True Alarm)
The Attention Signal — two tones at 853 and 960 Hz — was designed to cut through any acoustic environment, any emotional state, any dream. Tornado sirens modulate to approximate the doppler effect of approaching catastrophe. AMBER alerts occupy a distinct frequency band — a separate category of urgency. Nuclear test sirens descend because the blast wave has already passed. This composition uses the exact frequencies documented in FCC Public Notice DA 02-3580. The only modification is time. (Duration: approximately 3 minutes. The transformation is not an escape — it is a different way of hearing what has always been there.)
