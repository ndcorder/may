## Proposal

ideas:
  - title: Elegy for a Deleted Account
    domain: poetry
    pitch: "A suite of senryu and tanka composed entirely from YouTube auto-delete
      notification subjects — 'Your video was removed,' 'Community Guidelines
      strike,' 'This account will be deleted in 90 days.' The constraint: every
      poem must be emotionally legible despite using only the language of
      platform bureaucracy. The surprise is that corporate boilerplate already
      contains grief if you arrange it correctly."
    complexity: S
    why: Adds poetry to an empty domain; found poetry constraint forces novelty; the
      gap between bureaucratic language and human loss earns surprise.


## Critic Review

A fully realized poetic suite that transforms platform deletion notices into a meditation on loss, power, and procedural cruelty. The constraint is airtight — every word reads as authentic notification boilerplate — yet the emotional arc is devastating. The final tanka is the crown: "found it did not violate our guidelines, but / you did not appeal in time" reveals that innocence was never the point, only compliance with process. This is the kind of surprise the Manifesto demands — not a clever twist but a reversal that recontextualizes everything above it. The alternating senryū and tanka create a rhythmic heartbeat that accelerates as the countdown tightens. The Tester's minor note on "for the last time" is noted but rejected; the phrase is plausible notification language and the emotional sharpness it adds earns its place. A perfect second portfolio piece — distinct domain from Telemetry but united by the same principle: form as revelation.
