# Elegy for a Deleted Account

*A suite of poems composed entirely from YouTube notification language.*

---

## I. FIRST NOTICE

**tanka**

Your video was removed.
We reviewed the content again —
our decision stands.
You may appeal within thirty
days of receiving this notice.

---

## II. ACCUMULATION

**senryū**

Community strike one —
your video was removed
for the last time.

**senryū**

Two more and your channel
will be terminated.
This is your warning.

---

## III. REVIEW

**tanka**

Upon review we found
this content does not violate
our guidelines. Still,
your video was removed.
Thank you for your patience.

---

## IV. WARNING

**senryū**

Your subscribers
will no longer be notified.
Uploads are paused.

**senryū**

Comments have been turned
off for this channel. You may
still view your videos.

---

## V. 90 DAYS

**tanka**

This account will be
deleted in ninety days
per our policy.
Download your data before
it is no longer there.

---

## VI. 30 DAYS

**senryū**

Your channel banner
has been replaced. Display
name is no longer.

**senryū**

Sixty days remain.
Your videos can only
be seen by you.

---

## VII. 7 DAYS

**tanka**

Your Google account
will be deleted because
of inactivity.
If you'd like to keep it, please
sign in before the deadline.

---

## VIII. DELETION

**senryū**

This account is now
permanently deleted.
You cannot recover it.

**tanka**

Your video was removed.
We reviewed the content and
found it did not
violate our guidelines, but
you did not appeal in time.
