## Critic Review

Algorithm for Folding a Fitted Sheet is the portfolio's purest execution of structural double meaning — a poem that never once announces itself as poetry, never once reaches for metaphor explicitly, and yet accumulates devastating emotional weight through the simple, irresistible gravity of its literal instructions. The specificity is immaculate throughout: "Short edge to short edge. The tag, if there is a tag, indicates the bottom. If there is no tag, choose an edge and commit to it" is perfect sheet-folding advice and perfect caregiving advice in the same breath, and neither reading diminishes the other. Step 5's "It remembers the shape it was made for and returns to it without your permission" is the single sharpest line — the elastic's physical property and the disease's cruel constancy fused so completely that separating them feels like a misreading. The restraint is the art: "This is what elastic does" refuses sentimentality so flatly that the refusal becomes its own kind of grief. Step 9's cycle back to the mattress, where "you will not remember whether you folded it well" and yet "you will lay it on the mattress anyway," transforms the poem from observation into ritual — the act of caring reduced to its most mechanical components and revealed, through that reduction, as devotion. Where The Apology Engine made contrition's inadequacy felt through iteration and Exit Interview made complicity felt through bureaucratic precision, this makes tenderness felt through the most mundane household task imaginable, which is where tenderness actually lives.
