## EVERY GUN IN THIS ROOM

**$185,000 — 3BR/2BA — Quiet Street, Good Bones**

LIVING ROOM
Open concept. Vaulted ceilings with exposed
beam, original to the build. Hardwood under
carpet—verified. Southern exposure keeps it warm
through afternoon. The previous owner kept
a twelve-gauge behind the entry door, pump-action,
a ten-inch scratch in the stock where he'd leaned it
against the same wall each night for nineteen years.
Nice sightline to the front yard.

KITCHEN
Granite countertops, 2014. Tile backsplash
needs regrouting but the subfloor is solid.
Disposal hums. Fridge stays. The drawer second
from the stove pulls a little—something
inside it, heavy, shifts when you open it
all the way. Original hardware.
Eating area fits a four-person table.
Lots of light.

MASTER BEDROOM
Generous dimensions. Walk-in closet
with built-in organizers. Ceiling fan
with remote. His nightstand has a drawer
that doesn't close all the way—the Ruger
in its holster is a quarter-inch too tall.
Her side has a smaller piece, wrapped
in a washcloth, in the drawer with the phone
chargers and the orthodontist bill from 2019.
En suite updated.
Water pressure is excellent.

HALL BATH
Period tile, some cracking. Tub reglazed
two owners back. The grab bar someone installed
was removed and the holes filled with toothpaste—
not well. Medicine cabinet depth
is fourteen inches. The prescription bottles
are two years expired. The snub-nose
behind them has never been fired.
Good ventilation.

NURSERY
Currently used as an office.
Could easily convert back—room for a crib,
a changing table, a chair. The closet
is deep; previous tenants stored
a diaper bag on the hook behind the door.
Inside the diaper bag: wipes, three diapers,
a fully loaded .380 with the safety
mechanism that most people call a safety
because it makes them feel better about
the word "accident." Plush carpet,
recent. No stains.

HALLWAY
Long and narrow. Smoke detectors
hardwired, compliant. Two October tests
have blackened the paint below each unit.
The linoleum was chosen because it doesn't
hold sound. The previous family preferred it
when the children were sleeping, which was not
always, by then, the same as when the children
were quiet. Coat closet has an interior lock
installed at some point. Key not found.

GARAGE
Two-car. Epoxy floor, minor pooling
near the workbench where the chest freezer
hums in a way that sounds like breathing
from inside the house at 3 AM.
Pegboard wall—hammer, drill, levels,
a single nail where something heavier
once hung. The toolbox is locked
and was locked when we listed.
Original hardware.
Washer/dryer hookups.

BACKYARD
Fenced, chain-link. Mature tree, species
unknown—drops something in every season,
pods or leaves or small hard fruit, never
nothing. Slight grading issue near
the foundation. The soil tests high
in nitrogen, higher than the adjacent
lots. The neighbors don't mention it.
The neighbors don't mention
much about this property. Good fence.
Well-maintained.

But there were casings in the yard.
And a scratch on the molding by the garage
where the previous tenant kept a bag
between trips, at waist height.
And the holes in the bathroom
weren't for a grab bar.
The angle is wrong for a grab bar.
The angle is the angle
of someone standing, looking
at the face in the medicine cabinet,
reaching for something behind it
with a familiar hand.

The well-fed lawn.
The compliant detectors.
The linoleum that didn't hold sound.
The washcloth.
The orthodontist bill.

Everything about this house
was maintained.
